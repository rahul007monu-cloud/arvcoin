About

Not tips,
we teach the thinking

arvcoin is a market research and investor education platform. Structured analysis across stocks, F&O options, commodity, currency and crypto — always with the reasoning written out.

The problem

Millions of people enter the markets in India and start following "tips" from Telegram groups. Nobody knows what a call is based on. Screenshots of the winners circulate; the losers get deleted. Accuracy of 90% gets claimed, while the actual record exists nowhere.

The result: people learn to follow, not to understand.

What we do differently

🧠

Rationale, always

Every analysis explains why — structure, volume, level clusters, risk factors. Nothing is published without the reasoning.

📖

The record stays

Once published, a note cannot be edited or deleted. What worked and what did not — both stay on the record, visible to everyone.

🧮

The tool is free

The levels calculator is free forever — no login, no payment. Compute support, resistance, pivots and CPR yourself, and verify our work.

🚫

We do not sell guarantees

"Sure shot", "assured profit", "100% accuracy" — these phrases are technically blocked in our publishing system. Markets do not offer guarantees.

Where we stand on compliance

We state our registration status plainly on every page rather than burying it. We spell out the FEMA limits on forex and disclose the 30% tax on crypto. We do not give personalised advice, and we say so clearly.
