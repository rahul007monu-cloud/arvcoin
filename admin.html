Compliance reminders

Before every publish.

• Guaranteed, assured or fixed returns — never. Automatically blocked.

• Accuracy figures must come from the full history, never cherry-picked winners.

• A published note cannot be edited or deleted. Outcomes are updated with an audit trail.

• RA registration does not cover personalised advice — that requires IA registration.

• Currency: INR pairs on Indian exchanges only.

• Never describe ARV as an investment or an appreciating asset.
