The tracking identity

These two percentages are the same number by construction. If they ever diverge, the index is misconfigured — the overview page shows the locked launch reference used to compute it.

Market reference

Ethereum and Solana carry zero weight. They are here for context only and do not affect ARV.

Why there is no order book

ARV has no secondary market. Units are issued and redeemed against the index price, so there is no counterparty, no spread and no depth ladder to display. A bid/ask panel here would be invented. The chart above is real market data; anything that would have to be fabricated is absent rather than simulated.
