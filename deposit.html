How this works

  1. You get a UPI QR with the exact amount and a reference.
  2. You pay from any UPI app.
  3. You send us the UTR — the reference number your app shows — or a screenshot.
  4. We match it against the bank account and credit your balance.
Why the last step is manual. A UPI QR sends a request one way and returns nothing — scanning it tells your bank app what to do and tells us nothing at all. So we check the bank statement rather than trusting the screen. Usually 2–15 minutes.
