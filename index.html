01 — Mechanics

One formula, and that is the whole product

No committee sets the price. No operator adjusts it. Money arriving or leaving does not move it.

ARV(t) = — × BTC(t) ÷ BTC(launch)

Deposits cannot lift it

The price never reads how many units exist or how much money is held. New money issues new units at the current price rather than bidding it up, so an early holder gains nothing from a later one arriving. That is the difference between an index unit and a number somebody can push.

Quoted in rupees, deliberately

You pay in rupees and Bitcoin backs the unit, so ARV is quoted in rupees — which means its printed change is what your money actually did, currency movement included. Quote it in dollars and the headline would stop matching your bank statement.

Every trade at the index price

Orders are a queue, not an auction. No spread to cross and no haggling over price — whoever you are matched with, it settles at the index. A limit order is a trigger — act when ARV reaches this level — not an offer somebody has to accept.

02 — Buying and selling

You trade with a holder. The units sit in escrow.

Rupees move directly between you and the person on the other side. The units never do — they are held by us from the moment you match until the payment is confirmed, so neither side has to trust the other.

BuyPay the seller, get the units

You are matched with someone holding ARV. Their units go into escrow straight away, and you are shown where to pay them — their UPI ID or bank account. You pay from your own bank, enter the reference, and the escrow is released to you.

The price is the index price at the moment you matched. The seller does not set it and there is nothing to negotiate.

SellPaid into your own account

Your units move into escrow when a buyer matches, so they cannot be spent twice. The buyer pays the UPI ID or bank account you saved, you check the money has arrived, and you confirm the release.

A sell waits until a buyer takes it — there is no guaranteed instant exit. If a matched buyer does not pay inside the window, the escrow returns to you automatically and the order can be placed again.

Nothing is released on a promise. A seller confirms only after the money is in their account, and if they do not, the trade goes to us to settle rather than resolving itself in either direction. Proof of payment is attached to the trade and kept with it.
