01 — Mechanics

One formula, and that is the whole product

No committee sets the price. No operator adjusts it. Money arriving or leaving does not move it.

ARV(t) = ₹1 × BTC(t) ÷ BTC(launch)

The same number by construction. If these ever diverge, the index is misconfigured — which is why both are on screen rather than just one.

Deposits cannot lift it

The price never reads how many units exist or how much money is held. New money issues new units at the current price rather than bidding it up, so an early holder gains nothing from a later one arriving. That is the difference between an index unit and a number somebody can push.

Quoted in rupees, deliberately

You deposit rupees and the treasury holds Bitcoin, so ARV is quoted in rupees — which means its printed change is what your money actually did, currency movement included. Quote it in dollars and the headline would stop matching your statement.

Every fill at the index price

There is an order book, but it is a queue rather than an auction. No spread to cross, no haggling. A limit order is a trigger — act when ARV reaches this level — not an offer somebody has to accept.

02 — Buying and selling

Buy instantly. Sell to a real buyer, or to us.

BuyAlways instant

A buy fills against anyone waiting to sell, and the treasury covers the rest. You never queue for a seller to appear.

Pay by UPI into your rupee balance first, then buy with it — the same way an exchange works. Money never moves between users' bank accounts.

SellBuyer first, then the guarantee

A sell goes to a real buyer if one is waiting. If none is, it rests in the book — and after 60 minutes the treasury buys it at the index price.

That fallback exists because in a falling market everyone wants out and nobody wants in. Without it, holders would be stuck. With it, there is always an exit.

The 30% is not taken from your payout. Only the exit fee, GST and 1% TDS are withheld when you sell. The 30% plus 4% cess on your gain is your own liability at filing — the TDS already withheld counts against it. And losses on virtual digital assets cannot be set off against gains or carried forward, so two trades that cancel out can still leave tax owing. All of it is itemised before you confirm, and again in your tax statement.
