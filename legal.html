Risk Disclosure

Last updated:

⚠️
Investments are subject to market risk. Read all related documents carefully before investing. Only commit money you can afford to lose.

Equity (stocks)

Share prices fluctuate. Company performance, sector trends, macro conditions and sentiment all have an effect. Loss of capital is possible.

Derivatives — F&O, commodity, currency

Derivatives are leveraged. Losses can far exceed your initial margin, and your entire capital can be wiped out. SEBI studies show that a large majority of retail traders in F&O lose money. Options can expire worthless.

Commodity

Gold, silver, copper, zinc, crude oil, natural gas and agri contracts are affected by global supply and demand, geopolitics, currency moves and weather. Volatility can be extreme.

Currency

Currency derivatives move with central bank policy, interest rates and global flows. Indian residents may trade only RBI-approved INR pairs on recognised exchanges — offshore platforms breach FEMA.

Crypto (VDA)

Crypto is extremely volatile, falls outside SEBI's remit, and investor protection in India is limited. It is taxed at 30% plus 1% TDS. Value can fall to zero.

Limits of levels and technical analysis

Calculated levels come purely from the maths of historical price data. They account for no news, results, liquidity, gap openings or unexpected events. Levels are not guarantees — only reference points, and they can break.

No guaranteed returns

arvcoin promises no fixed, assured or guaranteed returns. Past performance is not a guarantee of future results.

Do your own research

Before taking any position, do your own analysis, understand your risk capacity, and consult a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser if needed.
