Risk Disclosure

Last updated:

⚠️
Investments are subject to market risk. Read all related documents carefully before investing. Sirf utna paisa lagao jitna aap kho sakte ho.

Equity (stocks)

Share prices upar-neeche hoti hain. Company performance, sector trend, macro conditions aur sentiment — sab asar karte hain. Capital ka nuksan ho sakta hai.

Derivatives — F&O, commodity, currency

Derivatives me leverage hota hai. Iska matlab nuksan aapke initial margin se bahut zyada ho sakta hai, aur poora capital doob sakta hai. SEBI ke studies batate hain ki F&O me trade karne wale retail traders ka bada hissa nuksan me rehta hai. Options expiry par worthless ho sakte hain.

Commodity

Gold, silver, copper, zinc, crude oil, natural gas aur agri contracts global supply-demand, geopolitics, currency movement aur weather se affect hote hain. Volatility bahut zyada ho sakti hai.

Currency

Currency derivatives central bank policy, interest rates aur global flows se move karte hain. Indian residents sirf RBI-approved INR pairs me, recognised exchanges pe trade kar sakte hain — offshore platforms FEMA ke against hain.

Crypto (VDA)

Crypto extremely volatile hai, SEBI ke dayre me nahi aata, aur India me investor protection framework limited hai. 30% tax + 1% TDS lagta hai. Value zero tak ja sakti hai.

Levels aur technical analysis ki limitation

Calculated levels sirf historical price data ke math se aate hain. Ye news, results, liquidity, gap openings ya kisi bhi unexpected event ko account me nahi lete. Levels guarantee nahi hain — sirf reference points hain jo break ho sakte hain.

No guaranteed returns

arvcoin koi fixed, assured ya guaranteed return promise nahi karta. Past performance future results ki guarantee nahi hai.

Apni research karo

Koi bhi position lene se pehle apni analysis karo, apni risk capacity samjho, aur zaroorat ho to SEBI-registered Investment Adviser se salah lo.

1. Nature of service

Subscription digital content aur research access ke liye hai. Payment ke turant baad access activate ho jaata hai aur content consume karna shuru ho jaata hai.

2. Cancellation

5. Process

Refund request support@arvcoin.com par bhejo — order ID aur reason ke saath. Hum 7 working days me review karke jawab denge. Approve hone par original payment method me 5–10 working days me credit ho jaata hai.
