Privacy Policy

Last updated: demo version

arvcoin ("hum") tumhari privacy ko seriously leta hai. Yeh policy batati hai ki hum kaunsa data collect karte hain aur kaise use karte hain.

1. Data hum collect karte hain

  • Account info: naam, email, mobile number
  • KYC data: PAN, Aadhaar (partner KYC ke liye, encrypted)
  • Transaction data: investment amount, assets, timestamps
  • Device & usage data: app performance behtar karne ke liye

2. Data ka use

Sirf service dene ke liye — account setup, KYC verification (partners ke through), transactions process karna, aur legal compliance. Hum tumhara data bechte nahi.

3. Sharing

Data sirf regulated partners (Razorpay, Onramp.money, CoinDCX, smallcase/brokers) ke saath share hota hai jitna transaction ke liye zaroori ho.

4. Security

256-bit encryption at rest & in transit. Access strictly controlled.

5. Tumhare rights

Tum apna data access, correct ya delete karwa sakte ho — support se contact karke.

Terms of Service

Last updated: demo version

arvcoin use karke tum in terms se agree karte ho. Dhyaan se padho.

2. Eligibility

Tumhari umar 18+ honi chahiye aur valid KYC (PAN/Aadhaar) hona chahiye.

3. Investments

4. Fees

5. Liability

Market losses ke liye arvcoin zimmedar nahi hai. Investments market risk ke adheen hain.

6. Changes

Hum terms update kar sakte hain; major changes pe notify karenge.
