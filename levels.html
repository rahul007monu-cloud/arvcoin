Chaar formulas kyun?

Classic, Fibonacci, Camarilla aur Woodie — har ek alag math use karta hai. Jahan multiple formulas ek hi level pe milte hain, wo cluster analysts ke liye interesting hota hai.

⚠️

Limitation

Ye pure math hai — news, results, global cues, liquidity kuch bhi account me nahi hai. Levels guarantee nahi hain, sirf reference points hain.
