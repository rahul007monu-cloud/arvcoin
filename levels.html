How to read these

◈

Pivot point

The average of the previous session’s high, low and close. Every other level is derived around this reference point.

▲

Resistance (R1–R4)

Computed levels above the pivot. These mark how far up the formulas extend.

▼

Support (S1–S4)

Computed levels below the pivot. These mark how far down the formulas extend.

◫

CPR

Central Pivot Range — the band formed by the pivot, TC and BC. Narrow and wide CPRs have historically behaved differently.

∑

Why four formulas?

Classic, Fibonacci, Camarilla and Woodie each use different maths. Where several formulas converge on the same level, that cluster tends to interest analysts.

⚠️

Limitation

This is pure maths — it accounts for no news, results, global cues or liquidity. Levels are not guarantees, only reference points.
