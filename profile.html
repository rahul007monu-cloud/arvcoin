Why the PAN matters

TDS under section 194S is withheld on the gross value of every redemption once your transfers for the year pass the threshold. With a PAN on record the rate is 1%. Without one, section 206AA raises it to 20% — twenty times as much money held back from each payout, all of it recoverable only by filing.
