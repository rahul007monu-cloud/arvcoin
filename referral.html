Fee tiers

—

Tiers pay in lower fees, not in cash. A cash reward that grows with how much money other people deposit is a return funded by deposits, which is the shape of a scheme rather than a business — and it is a deposit-taking offence under the BUDS Act, 2019. A fee discount comes out of our own margin, so it is ours to give.

What it does not do

  • No second level. You are not paid on the people they invite.
  • No cut of their trading, ever — only that one first deposit.
  • Nothing is taken from them. The commission is ours, not theirs.
  • Self-referral is void, and a duplicate PAN or device voids both sides.

Tax on this

Referral commission is ordinary income at your slab rate, not a capital gain. It is kept apart from your ARV holdings and does not change their cost basis, so selling ARV later is unaffected by it. It shows in your tax statement under other income.
