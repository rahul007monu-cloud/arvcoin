What the no-set-off rule cost you

s.115BBH

How this is computed

  • Cost of acquisition only. Entry and exit fees, GST and any other expense are not deductible. Only what you paid for the units reduces the gain.
  • FIFO. The oldest units you hold are treated as sold first, which fixes the cost against each redemption.
  • Gains are not netted against losses. Each redemption stands alone. A loss cannot reduce another gain and cannot be carried into a later year.
  • 30% plus 4% cess on the gain — 31.2% effective. Flat, regardless of your slab or holding period.
  • 1% TDS is withheld on gross proceeds once your transfers for the year pass ₹10,000. It is a credit, not an extra tax.
This is a computation from your recorded transactions, not a filing and not tax advice. Verify the rates and the treatment with a chartered accountant before you file — and give them the CSV rather than a screenshot.
