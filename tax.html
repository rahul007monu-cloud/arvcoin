How these numbers are worked out

Gains are taxed at a flat rate. Section 115BBH taxes income from transferring a virtual digital asset at 30% plus 4% health and education cess, whatever your slab. There is no long-term rate and no indexation.
Only cost of acquisition is deductible. Not our fee, not the GST on it, not anything else. So the fees shown on this page are real money you paid but they do not reduce the taxable gain.
Losses cannot be set off, or carried forward. Section 115BBH(2) blocks both — against other VDA gains, against any other income, and into the next year. That is why the figure above is the sum of your gains rather than gains minus losses.
Cost basis is first-in, first-out. A sale consumes your oldest units first, and the cost recorded against it is the cost of exactly those units. It is fixed at the moment of the sale, so a later purchase never changes an earlier disposal.
TDS is a credit, not an extra tax. Section 194S makes us withhold 1% of the gross consideration on your sales and pay it to the government against your PAN. It reduces what you owe at filing, and it appears in Form 26AS. Without a PAN on record, section 206AA raises the rate to 20% — which is why the PAN field is worth filling in.
The threshold is an aggregate, not an allowance. Once your total sale proceeds for the year pass the threshold, the whole of a transfer is liable — not merely the part above the line.
Referral commission is not a capital gain. It is ordinary income at your slab rate, kept apart from your ARV holdings so it never contaminates their cost basis.
