How a fill works here
  • Buys are instant. They fill against anyone waiting to sell, and the treasury covers the rest. You never queue.
  • Sells look for a buyer first. If none is waiting the order rests — and after 60 minutes the treasury buys it, so there is always an exit.
  • A limit order is a trigger, not an offer. It acts when ARV reaches your level, at the index price.
  • 1% TDS is withheld on every sale. The 30% plus cess on the gain is not withheld — it is yours to pay at filing.
