How a trade works here
  • Buy: place the order, match with a real seller, pay them directly on the UPI shown, upload proof — the seller confirms and your ARV is released from escrow.
  • Sell: list your ARV — a buyer pays you on the UPI you set in your profile, and you confirm receipt to release the units.
  • Limit / stop-loss / target are price triggers on the live index. A sell stop-loss protects the downside; a target locks in a gain.
  • Timers: an unmatched order stays open 24h; after a match the buyer has 60 minutes to pay; after proof, the seller has 4 hours to confirm — otherwise it goes to support.
