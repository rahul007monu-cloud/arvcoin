Selling is a separate step

Withdrawing moves rupees out. It is not a sale, so there is no tax on it — the tax happened when you sold ARV, and it is itemised on that trade.

1% TDS is withheld at the moment of a sale under section 194S. The 30% plus cess on the gain is your own liability at filing, and your tax statement totals it for the year.
