Two different things

TDS is withheld now — 1% of the gross value under section 194S. You do not receive it; it is credited against your eventual liability and appears in your Form 26AS.

The 30% plus 4% cess is not withheld. It is your own liability under section 115BBH, payable when you file your return. This app computes and reports it so it is not a surprise later. Note that platform fees are not deductible against it, and losses cannot be set off or carried forward.
